01 Program structure

Three steps, one shelf.

The same simple path leads to every tier — only how far a store goes changes the reward.

1

Stock the Core 13

All 13 mandatory products, on shelf, no exceptions. This is the entry ticket for every tier.

2

Add from the bonus range

Layer on extra SKUs from the tier lists — the more a store stocks, the higher the tier it reaches.

3

Earn cash, tier by tier

Qualify for anywhere from £10 to £20 in cash, paid per qualification period.
